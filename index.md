I am currently a PhD student at UCLA advised by [Prof. Cho-Jui Hsieh](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~chohsieh/), and a project lead at [TurningPointAI](https://www.turningpoint-ai.com/), advised by [Ruochen Wang](https://ruocwang.github.io/), [Prof. Minhao Cheng](https://cmhcbb.github.io/), [Prof. Tianyi Zhou](https://tianyizhou.github.io/teaching/). We are a collaborative initiative dedicated to advancing the field of Multimodal Language Agents. Learn more about our work at [TurningPointAI](https://www.turningpoint-ai.com/) and stay updated by following us on [twitter](https://x.com/TurningPointAI). Previously, I was fortunate to work with [Prof. Yue Gao](https://www.gaoyue.org/) on 3D Computer Vision at Tsinghua University. 

My research is funded by Amazon Trainium Fellowship. For further information, please see my [CV](https://hengguangzhou.github.io/assets/files/cv.pdf) (last update: Nov 23, 2024).

**Research Interests**: My research centers on advancing **MLLM post-training**, with a focus on **Reasoning/Agent** and **Multimodal**.
- **Reasoning/Agent**: Eliciting the reasoning and agentic capabilities of (M)LLMs via RL ([VisualThinker](https://github.com/turningpoint-ai/VisualThinker-R1-Zero/)).
- **Multimodal**: Exploring how models comprehend and interpret the physical world across diverse sensory ([VisualThinker](https://github.com/turningpoint-ai/VisualThinker-R1-Zero/), [MOSSBench](https://github.com/xirui-li/MOSSBench)).

I developed [**VisualThinker**](https://github.com/turningpoint-ai/VisualThinker-R1-Zero/), one of the first open-source repo to replicate the aha moment of Deepseek-r1 on a small non-sft multimodal model(600+ Github stars).

Prior to the era of LLM, I had experiences working on 3D Computer Vision, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and visually-rich document understanding.
